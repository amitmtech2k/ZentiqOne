Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. This page explains what information ZentiqOne collects through this website and how it is used.

Short version: ZentiqOne does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site. The only personal information collected is what you choose to submit through the Contact or Submit a Review forms, and it is used solely to respond to you or, for reviews, to display the feedback you've given permission to publish.

Information We Collect

When you submit the Contact form, we collect the details you provide: name, company, designation, email, phone number, industry, services of interest, your message, project stage, timeline, preferred contact method, and (if you choose) a preferred meeting date and time. We also automatically capture the page you submitted from, the referring page, a timestamp, and your IP address and browser user agent, which help us respond appropriately and filter automated spam submissions.

When you submit a review through the Submit a Review form, we collect your rating, review text, name, title, company, email, and phone number, along with the permissions you select for how that review may be used (displayed publicly, used in marketing, or translated).

We do not collect information through cookies, analytics tools, or any other passive tracking on this site.

How We Use Your Information

Contact form submissions are used to respond to your enquiry and, where you've consented, to follow up about ZentiqOne's services. Review submissions are moderated before publication; only reviews you've given permission to display publicly (and that pass moderation) appear on the Success Stories page, showing the fields you agreed to share.

How Your Information Is Processed

Form submissions are sent through Brevo, a third-party email and customer communication platform, which ZentiqOne uses to receive and manage enquiries and review submissions. Brevo processes this data on ZentiqOne's behalf and maintains its own security and privacy safeguards.

Data Retention

We retain contact and review submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain a record of published reviews, or comply with legal obligations. You can request that we delete your information at any time (see Your Rights below).

Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information, including removal of a published review.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing follow-up at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at zentiqone@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to this website or applicable law. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to zentiqone@gmail.com.

This policy is provided as a general reference and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional on applicable data protection law.
