# Homepage Redesign Brief

Research-backed direction for zentiqone.com's homepage, step one of the redesign (homepage first, per your call). This is a direction document, not code yet, next step after this is agreeing on the specifics below, then wireframes.

## What real comparable sites do differently

Looked at Setu, M2P Fintech, Zeta, Perfios, Signzy (Indian BaaS/payments infra, closest comps to ZentiqOne), plus Stripe, Plaid, and Modern Treasury for contrast.

**Hero pattern**: every one of them uses one headline plus one subheadline that adds new information, never a restatement. Stripe: "Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue" then a subhead about mechanism, not a second sentence repeating "financial infrastructure." Setu's hero is two words: "Full-stack fintech."

**Proof above the fold**: near-universal. Either a logo strip of real named clients (Setu shows Kotak, ICICI, CRED; Modern Treasury shows 33 named customers) or a sourced, specific metric (Plaid: "1 in 2 banked adults in the U.S. use Plaid"). Nobody leads with an unsourced round number like "18+ integrations."

**The one closest to ZentiqOne's actual situation is Perfios**: zero client logos, zero testimonials, zero named case studies on their homepage either. Instead of inventing outcomes they can't yet claim, they state specific, checkable technical scope: "250+ Financial Institutions supported," "1000+ E-Statement formats supported." Specificity about capability substitutes for proof about outcomes.

**Layout variety**: alternating image-text modules instead of uniform card grids, tabs or horizontal scroll for long lists instead of another 3-column grid, logo marquees instead of static chip walls.

## What this means for ZentiqOne specifically

**Hero**: cut the badge, headline, lead paragraph, and definition-block down from three overlapping statements to one headline plus one subhead that says something the headline didn't already say. The definition-block I already edited (positioning as "not a bank/PA/PG") is a reasonable stopgap; longer term it should carry new information, not repeat the lead.

**Replace invented stats with checkable specifics**: "18+ Banking & Network Integrations" and "8-Stage Delivery Process" read exactly like the Perfios problem, unsourced round numbers with nothing behind them. llms.txt already lists the real, specific integrations: NPCI, Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, CBS, AML, CKYC, DigiLocker, Aadhaar, UPI, NACH, FASTag, AEPS. That list is more credible than "18+" because it's specific and checkable. Lead with it.

**Kill "Coming Soon" on Success Stories**: a placeholder sitting next to confident-sounding stats undercuts both. Either write one real, specific engagement (anonymized if needed: "helped a licensed PA design its escrow release architecture") once Amit can describe one, or drop the stats bar's unverifiable numbers until there's something to back them.

**Cut the buzzword walls**: the 15-chip "Technology Stack" list and 12-chip "Featured Solutions" list are what every dev shop's site has. Replace with the specific integrations list above, which nobody else can copy-paste because it's what ZentiqOne actually does.

**Break the grid rhythm**: the homepage is currently about ten sections in a row, each a 3 or 4-column card grid or a chip row. At least one section (the service breakdown, most likely) should become an alternating image-text or tabbed layout instead of another grid, so the page doesn't scroll like a slide deck.

**Credibility substitute until real testimonials exist**: submit-review.html will eventually supply real testimonials, but until then, avoid a placeholder testimonials section. A founder-credibility anchor (Amit's specific background, LinkedIn, years in the space) or naming actual compliance frameworks the work is designed around (only ones that are true, do not list certifications ZentiqOne doesn't hold) does more than an empty grid.
